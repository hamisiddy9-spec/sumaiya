# TemplateHub Teardown — Marketing × Engineering × The Payer

*Compiled 2026-09-07 from live production checks + full codebase. Severities reflect prod at time of writing.*

Lenses: **[MKT]** marketing / a stranger deciding whether to trust it · **[PAY]** the non-technical person about to spend TZS 7,200 · **[ENG]** the engineer keeping it running.

---

## 00 · The one finding everything else hangs off

**The free tier works. The paid tier is offline.**

- The parts you give away — 8 PDF tools, ATS checker, 260+ templates, PDF export — are healthy and useful.
- The parts you charge for — *"AI content suggestions", tailoring, cover letters, CV import, interview coach* — are **100% down in production**. DeepSeek (the AI provider) is out of credits; every AI call errors.
- There is no working way to pay even if AI worked: PayMe Africa returns `401 Invalid Signature` on every request (unresolved), the checkout UI is off, yet `PAYMENTS_ENABLED=true` is still set on the server from a debugging session — a half-on state.

**So: a visitor cannot become a paying customer, and if they could, they'd be paying for a feature that errors.** None of this is architectural — the AI outage is one env var, the payment bug is a PayMe support ticket, the positioning problems are copy.

## 01 · The money path — can anyone actually pay?

**[PAY][ENG] BLOCKER — Four things stand between "interested" and "paid":**
1. AI features error — the thing the plans sell.
2. PayMe signature rejected — no transaction completes.
3. `NEXT_PUBLIC_PAYMENTS_ENABLED` off — no checkout button anywhere.
4. A "launch period — everything is free" banner on the pricing page telling people not to bother.
→ **Do:** Decide if you're taking money this month. If yes: fix AI, crack the PayMe signature (get their support to send the exact HMAC construction + a worked example), flip both flags, drop the banner. If no: `PAYMENTS_ENABLED=false`, remove the `_diag` route + `PAYME_DIAG_KEY`, stop half-maintaining a payment system.

**[MKT][PAY] CONTRADICTION — Is the PDF free or not? The site says both.**
Landing + FAQ: *"download a polished PDF for free."* Pricing page: *"Everything except downloading… You only pay when you want to save a PDF."* A cautious buyer notices this at the payment moment and leaves.
→ **Do:** Pick one model. Cleanest for this market: **PDF free always** (it's your acquisition promise + SEO hook), charge for AI + unlimited docs + Word export + ATS history. Rewrite pricing around that.

**[PAY] RIGHT — Payment methods and price points fit the market.**
M-Pesa / Airtel / Tigo USSD push + card via PayMe. ~$2.90–7.90 shown in TZS/KES. The mobile-money flow, when it works, feels native to this user in a way a Stripe redirect never would.

**[MKT] UNVALIDATED — Nobody has ever paid.**
The app has been free the whole time; the entire paid model is untested. Before investing more in payment plumbing, get 10 people to say "yes I'd pay TZS 7,200 for that" — or run a founding-member test the moment AI is back.

**[MKT][PAY] BIGGEST LEVER — Zero social proof anywhere on the site.**
No testimonials, no "12,000 CVs made", no rating, no "featured in", no named user. Survivable for a free tool; fatal for asking a cautious person in Nairobi/Dar for TZS 7,200. This market buys on trust and word of mouth, and the page gives them nothing.
→ **Do:** 3–5 real testimonials (first name, photo, specific outcome like "got 3 interviews in two weeks"). Live-ish CV-count in the hero. Move the 48h money-back guarantee from footer to under the CTA. One-day job, highest conversion payoff on the list.

**[MKT] UPSIDE — The PDF tools are a bigger funnel than they're used as.**
"merge pdf" / "jpg to pdf" = enormous permanent search volume, and a big share are job seekers assembling an application. They land, succeed, and see nothing pointing at the CV builder. Every tool result page should end with "Now build a CV that gets read — free" + a template strip.

## 03 · "Will a non-technical person get it — and pay?"

*Your words: "idiots too — they are the ones who will pop the cash."*

**[PAY] GOOD — The front door is genuinely simple now.**
One plain headline, a 3-step "how it works", one big button, a CV preview on the phone. Understood in five seconds. Wasn't true before.

**[PAY] FRICTION — "Fill it in yourself" assumes they know what a good CV looks like. They don't.**
The reason this user needs help is they can't write "Results-driven engineer with 6+ years…" from a blank box. The AI was the answer — and it's down. A first-timer lands in the editor, sees empty fields, no idea what to type.
→ **Do:** Example placeholder text in every field, a "see a filled example" toggle, section-by-section prompts even without AI.

**[PAY] FRICTION — 260 templates is a decision they can't make.**
→ **Do:** One recommended template by default ("we picked Nakuru for you — change any time"). Filters by role + career stage turn 260 into 8.

**[PAY][ENG] DROP-OFF — The magic-link sign-in wall loses people at the worst moment.**
Built the CV → hit Download → "check your email" → app switch → distraction → a real fraction never return. Google is faster, but the sign-in page still doesn't handle "I made my account with Google, I have no password" — it shows a password error instead of detecting the Google-only account.
→ **Do:** Test "download, we'll email you the PDF, sign in later to edit" vs the wall. Instrument this exact step — almost certainly your biggest leak.

**[PAY] GOOD — Swahili throughout + mobile-money payment are real advantages for this buyer.**
A Kiswahili-first UI and an M-Pesa PIN prompt are what makes this feel trustworthy and native. No generic international CV builder does this. Lean into it harder in the marketing — it's a moat, not a footnote.

## 04 · The AI (Sumaiya)

**[ENG][MKT] DOWN — Single provider, no credit alerting, no fallback key — so it failed silently for weeks.**
Everything AI routes through one function to DeepSeek. The code has a fallback chain to Anthropic/OpenAI but no keys set. When DeepSeek hit zero balance, chat / tailor / cover letters / CV import / interview coach all errored — nothing alerted anyone. Discovered by inspection, not monitoring.
→ **Do:** Set `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` (or `OPENAI_API_KEY`) in Vercel prod so the fallback kicks in. Add a balance/error alert. ~1 hour, highest-value hour on this document.

**[PAY] BUG — The Snap job-description guard rejects real job postings.**
A legitimate one-paragraph JD was rejected with `NOT_A_JOB` — "This doesn't look like a job description." A real posting getting bounced makes the feature look broken. Anti-fabrication intent right, threshold too tight.
→ **Do:** Loosen it, or downgrade a borderline case from hard reject to a soft "this looks short — paste the full posting for a better result" that still proceeds.

**[ENG][PAY] UPSIDE — Model quality vs. price, and streaming.**
DeepSeek is the cheap choice, defensible for a free tier. Someone paying $8 for "AI content suggestions" may expect GPT-4-class output — consider routing the paid tier (or "deep" thinking effort) to a stronger model. If the chat doesn't stream tokens, a 10–30s wait behind a typing dot reads as frozen — SSE streaming works fine on Vercel's Node runtime.

**[ENG] STRONG — The anti-fabrication engineering is a genuine moat when it's running.**
Evidence engine, JD guard, repeat-guard, "grounded in your facts, never invents results", bilingual chat, skill-gap analysis with verified course links, interview coach. A much more serious AI product than the competition — the marketing should say so specifically once it's back.

## 05 · Templates

**[MKT][ENG] UNAUDITED CLAIM — "Every template passes ATS" — has anyone checked?**
You ship a deterministic ATS checker. Run every template's sample output through it, publish the score on each template page ("ATS score: 94" is a great trust signal), retire/fix any below ~85. Right now it's an assertion; it could be a proof.

**[PAY] CURATION — 260+ with no guidance is a liability, not an asset.**
Volume looks impressive in a headline and is paralysing in practice. Curate: "Top 12", filters for "first job / mid-career / senior / creative / academic", "most downloaded this week". The web CLAUDE.md also notes a web-vs-mobile count mismatch (273 vs 266) — reconcile it.

## 06 · Engineering — architecture & debt

**[ENG] SOUND — One API for web + mobile, Supabase for auth/db/storage.**
Hono on Vercel Fluid Compute, shared logic, no separate mobile backend. Correct architecture, scales fine. Don't let anyone talk you into microservices.

**[ENG][PAY] DATA/TRUST — `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` empty in prod — "delete account" leaves a ghost.**
Without it, deletion removes DB rows but not the Supabase auth record. The person can still "sign in" to an empty account, and their email is still on file after they asked you to erase it. Privacy-promise violation, possibly legal.
→ **Do:** Add the key to Vercel prod (15 min). Backfill-delete auth users whose profile rows are already gone.

**[ENG] DEBRIS — Debugging scaffolding left in production.**
`/api/payments/_diag` + `PAYME_DIAG_KEY` were added to reverse-engineer the PayMe signature and never removed. Guarded, but an unnecessary attack-surface footnote that hits PayMe's sandbox on demand. Delete both.

**[ENG] BLIND — No error tracking — the AI outage proves it.**
PostHog captures product analytics, but a multi-week total AI failure went unnoticed. Add Sentry on the API. First alert: any 5xx from `/api/ai/*` and `/api/payments/*`.

**[ENG] FIXED THIS SESSION — CI merge gate, lockfile drift, native app split.**
Eval + verify workflows were red on every push for ~a week (root `npm ci` choking on a mobile-dep-drifted lockfile). Fixed by removing `apps/*` from root workspaces. Native app retired to its own local project + fully rebuilt. `next build` no longer type-checks dead code. Master is green.

**[ENG] UNTESTED — The native app has never touched a device this cycle.**
Rebuilt end-to-end — clean typecheck, green bundle, 60 passing unit tests — but camera, on-device OCR, PDF export, share sheet, deep links, auth round-trip unverified on hardware. No E2E coverage of payment or AI paths on web either.

## 07 · Native app — post-rebuild

**[ENG][PAY] REBUILT — Every screen now runs on a real design system.**
Primitives (Button, TextField, Screen, Card, Skeleton, EmptyState, ListRow, haptics, theme) — none existed before. Animated tab bar, screen transitions, spring-in splash, swipeable onboarding. Auth with inline validation + real Google logo. Camera scan with document-frame overlay. Multi-section CV editor (add/remove experience, education, skills) where there were two fields. Settings merged into Profile (4 tabs). No emoji-as-icon, no dead hover classes, no deprecated components.

**[ENG] GATE — Local-only, unproven on hardware — do not ship yet.**
Lives at `~/templatehub-native`, no remote, not published. Before any store submission: full device pass of every flow, decide `vendor/` vs shared-package strategy, wire real CI. AI-dependent screens inherit the prod AI outage.

## 08 · Fix list, ranked

*By revenue impact × severity ÷ effort.*

1. **Restore the AI** *(~1 hr)* — Set `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` in Vercel prod (fallback chain exists) or top up DeepSeek, add an error alert. Nothing else matters while the paid feature errors and the marketing promises it.
2. **Fix `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` in prod** *(~15 min)* — Ghost accounts after "delete" is a privacy violation. Add the key, backfill-delete orphans.
3. **Resolve the free-vs-paid PDF contradiction** *(~half day)* — Recommend: PDF always free, charge for AI + Word + unlimited. Rewrite pricing, kill the "everything free" banner, align every FAQ.
4. **Add social proof to landing + pricing** *(~1 day)* — Highest conversion payoff. 3–5 real testimonials with names/photos/outcomes, a CV-count in the hero, 48h guarantee under the CTA.
5. **Decide payments: finish or fully disable** *(depends on PayMe)* — Get PayMe support to send the exact signature algorithm + a worked example. Until then: `PAYMENTS_ENABLED=false`, remove `_diag` + `PAYME_DIAG_KEY`.
6. **Instrument the funnel** *(~2 hrs)* — PostHog funnel on: landed → started CV → hit download wall → signed in → downloaded. Watch a week. Stop guessing where people leave.
7. **Make the empty editor usable without AI** *(~1 day)* — Example placeholder text every field, "see a filled CV" toggle, section prompts. The first-timer can't write from a blank box — and that's who pays.
8. **Curate templates** *(~2 days)* — Default pick in the editor, role/level filters, "Top 12", self-audit + publish ATS scores, reconcile web/mobile count.
9. **Loosen the Snap JD guard + link PDF tools to the builder** *(~2 hrs)* — Stop rejecting real job posts. Add a "now build your CV" CTA to every PDF tool result.
10. **Add error tracking, then device-test the native app** *(~1 hr + ~1 day)* — Sentry on `/api/ai/*` and `/api/payments/*` first. Then a full hardware pass before the mobile app goes near a store.
