Muundo wa Kiufundi · System & Wiring Blueprint

Behavior & wiring manual

The four screens above are the design. This section is the contract: what each element does when it is touched, where the state lands, and which rules are enforced by a test rather than by goodwill.

1 · The inviolable laws

Law 1 · CI check

Never “ATS” in Kiswahili copy

The Swahili surface says Ukaguzi wa CV, and findings are grouped strictly as Kikubwa, Wastani, Kidogo. English surfaces may keep the acronym where an English reader expects it.

Law 2 · CI check

No pure white as a surface

Base #FBF9F5, raised #F5F3EF, sunken #EFEEEA. Espresso dark: #1B1C1A / #24201C / #141210. White is for text and icons on ember, nothing else.

Law 3 · CI check

No fabricated data or scores

A score appears only when a real evaluation produced it. An empty draft gets the honest line, not a zero. Counts are dynamic or read Miundo yote; a literal template count is illegal.

Law 4 · CI check

House mark, 3 blades at 120°

Opacities 1.0 / 0.9 / 0.8, wells at 128dp (hero), 96dp (dialogs), 80dp (bars). Never redrawn, never recoloured, never rotated.

Law 5 · Held by people, not by a test

Memorial dignity

Sumaiya is named in loving memory of the founder’s late sister. Microcopy stays warm, respectful and empowering. No cynical tech jargon, no humour at the user’s expense, nothing a family would wince at. This is the one law a linter cannot check, so it is written here on purpose.

4 · Offline-first pipeline

[ input event ]

↓

[ zod validate ]

↓

[ local write <2ms ] → IndexedDB (web) / expo-sqlite (phone)

↓

[ micro-toast: “Imehifadhiwa (Otomatiki)” ] — confirmation of a completed write

↓

[ network health ]

online → drain outbox → PUT delta → server broadcast

offline → queue in outbox, keep working

The local write is the save. The upload is a copy. That ordering is what makes the product usable on a phone with no bundle.

5 · Extension runtime contract (Manifest V3)

Verified domains only

The content script mounts on an explicit allow-list of job domains and nowhere else. No ambient reading of the user’s browsing.

Scoring is local

Cosine similarity against the CV cached on the device. The advert text is not uploaded to be scored, so the score reflects the user’s real fields or it is absent.

Autofill never invents

Form injection writes the user’s stored answers. A field with no answer is left for the user — a guessed value in a job application is worse than an empty one.

The original is preserved

A tailored CV is a clone. The user’s master document is never edited in place by a one-click action.

6 · Export mechanics

Single file, no network. This document carries its own stylesheet and its fonts. Open it on a train, on an intranet, in five years — it renders the same.

Print isolation. @media print locks the document canvas to ISO A4 (210×297mm) with margins fixed, so paper output cannot drift from the preview.

Four viewports, one artefact. Desktop, tablet, mobile and extension are the same components at different breakpoints — not four separate designs that must be kept in sync by hand.
