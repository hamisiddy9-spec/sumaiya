1

Your message says when it left

One word, right-aligned, in the bubble: Imetumwa. It is the cheapest possible delivery receipt and it is the difference between “did that go?” and knowing. The other two states are Inatuma… and Haijatumwa · Jaribu tena — and the words are never lost: the bubble stays exactly where it is while it retries.

2

Her name appears once per run, not once per bubble

A 20 dp mark + SUMAIYA above the first of her bubbles. Her replies arrive in pieces (reply-bubbles.ts, 550 ms apart) — announcing each one would be a stutter.

3

The wait leaves a receipt instead of vanishing

One collapsible line: “Nilifanya hivi: hatua 3”. Tapping it replays the steps that were shown while she worked (screen 04). This replaces the three-dot bubble that today disappears with nothing behind it.

4

The document card, and why it is not a bubble

The live app renders CvPreviewCard inside the transcript (chat-message.tsx:119-137) with the template hard-coded to classic and no cvId carried into the editor (sumaiya-chat-screen.tsx:400-402). The card here names the object, states what she used and what she could not find, and carries identity both ways.

5

A save control that is never greyed out

This is the snap-to-apply dead end answered directly: saving from the chat writes locally first, so the button always completes, and the state line under it reports the truth — Imehifadhiwa kwenye CV zako with “inatumika bila mtandao”. If the server push fails later, the line becomes Imehifadhiwa · inasubiri mtandao, never a lie.

6

Three suggestions, named as outcomes

No pronouns, no feature labels: Lenga kwenye kazi · Kagua CV yako · Barua ya maombi. Today the third-person chrome label Msaidizi wa AI and the first-person feature AI ya kazi sit elsewhere in the app; a chip never has to choose a person because it names a result.

7

Entrances: 300 ms, one stagger, nothing over 270 ms

Bubbles rise 300ms ease-out at 30/70/110 ms. The document card is the only element with a slightly different entrance — scale .985→1 + 6dp rise, 220ms — because a produced thing should read as placed on the table, not as another line of speech.

What this replaces

The bare CvPreviewCard in the transcript (and, for the same job, the floating “Tengeneza CV yako kutoka mazungumzo haya” bar at sumaiya-chat-screen.tsx:450-477, which appears after two turns and then never mentions the CV again). The card absorbs that bar: the offer becomes the artifact.
