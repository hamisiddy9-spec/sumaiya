Why four, and which four

The four that map to what this product is for: see the state (Nyumbani), write the document (CV), find the opening (Ajira), track it (Maombi). Muhtasari wa siku, Mahojiano, Kozi, Mipangilio and Premium are real but occasional, so they live in Zaidi — and they are still named there, never reduced to a bare icon.

Why the assistant is a pill, not a fifth tab

On desktop the assistant is a destination and a mode: you may want it beside what you are looking at rather than instead of it. A pill separates it from the four peers without spending a fifth slot. It is also the honest reading of the existing web app, where Sumaiya is already a top-level destination.

The marketing surface already has its own nav (marketing-nav.tsx) with a logo + destinations + CTA shape. It keeps that role, and the signed-in bar must not leak into it. In this band the labels drop, never the objects: Ajira and Maombi fold into Zaidi, the assistant keeps its pill and its name, and the account keeps its avatar. Nothing becomes an icon-only mystery item — every collapsed destination is still named inside Zaidi.

Kwa nini

A 768px window cannot hold four words plus an assistant without crowding the thumb zone or wrapping. Two primaries plus the assistant is the number that survives translation: Swahili labels are longer than their English originals (Nyumbani, Mipangilio, Mahojiano).

DECISION

Labels drop before objects do

Two primaries (Nyumbani, CV) plus the assistant and Zaidi. The reason is measurable, not aesthetic: Swahili labels are longer than their English originals, so a four-word bar wraps or crowds earlier here than it would in English. Nothing is ever an unlabelled icon. The account keeps its avatar at every width, because "who am I signed in as" is not a finding-a-destination task.

AUDIT · dashboard/mobile-tab-bar.tsx:81

the web already has a phone tab bar

mobile-tab-bar.tsx exists and is md:hidden. It is already the right idea; what it is not yet is the same five slots as the APK. If the labels or the order differ, a user who has both surfaces learns two navigations for one product — and docs/AREAS-AND-PARITY-2026-09-20.md names exactly this drift as the failure mode to prevent.

THE RULE

one product, one set of five

  • Same five labels, same order, same icons as app-tabs.tsx:1-146: Nyumbani · CV · Sumaiya · Pakia · Wasifu.
  • The active state is the same weight change, not a different treatment per platform.
  • The mobile-web top bar holds only the logo and the account, so it is not a second nav competing for the same thumb zone.
  • Its height clears env(safe-area-inset-bottom) exactly as the APK does; globals.css already defines .safe-bottom-nav and .pb-tabbar.
HONEST LIMIT

this does not make the web app the product

The APK is the product for this user. The web nav matters because a link shared on WhatsApp lands here first — and WhatsApp is the connective tissue in this market. So the mobile-web nav's job is to be recognisable, then hand the user to the APK, not to compete with it.
