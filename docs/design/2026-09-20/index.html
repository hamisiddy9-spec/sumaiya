How to read a mockup

›The mockups

00
Mfumo wa kubuni · The design system
Nine type roles, the 4px spacing scale, concentric radii, the elevation split between the content plane and the floating layer, three icon sizes, and the motion additions + ban list. Includes the measured contrast finding that drives the ember-as-text rule.
01
Nyumbani (home) · Home / dashibodi
Before and after side by side. The advert leaves the first slot, saved-state becomes visible, quick actions halve in height, and the loading boundary stops popping. Approved by the owner as the reference for the system’s direction.
02
Kihariri cha CV · The document editor
Section rail to jump between form sections, an autosave state the user can see, and the PDF export moved into the thumb zone.
03
Ajira · The jobs feed
One entry point for the eye, a score badge that stops meaning two things, and a corrected note: the live device proves this feed is populated, and that a new account sees the unmatched list by default.
04
Wasifu · Profile
Who is signed in, one scored prompt that explains why matching is weak, and destructive actions ordered by how recoverable they are. The device confirmed the DANGER ZONE split.
05
Hali tupu / kupakia / hitilafu · Empty, loading and error
One composite, three faces. The skeleton keeps the geometry of what replaces it; the error tells the user what is still available before asking them to retry.
06
Sanduku la mazungumzo · The chat box
Four starter chips cut to two, the artefact rendered in the thread that produced it, and the composer held still because typing must stay instant. The assistant itself is live (DeepSeek), so nothing here assumes a broken one.
07
Upau wa urambazaji wa wavuti · Web navigation
The nav that was never designed: four primaries + the assistant + Zaidi on desktop, the signed-out bar, the narrow-desktop band, and a mobile-web bar that mirrors the APK’s five slots instead of fighting them.
08
Lugha moja ya madirisha · One overlay language
Five variants — bottom sheet, confirm dialog, toast, full-screen sheet, share sheet — plus the audit that shows three native sheet radii, six web scrims and seven z-indices where the theme already defines one.
09
Njia ya kukaribisha · The welcoming sequence
Launch to first arrival as ONE sequence: the audit of what each welcoming screen does differently (four origins, thirteen durations, five easing families), the six divergences named, and the hand-off that fixes “different elements stacked together”.

›Honesty notes

WHAT THESE ARE NOT

Mockups, not a shipped change

No source file was modified. Every proposal here is a description tied to a current file:line; the mockups are HTML that demonstrates the target, and the phone frames may show slightly different text lengths than a real 360dp budget phone would give.

CORRECTED ON THE LIVE DEVICE

the job feed is NOT empty — this document was wrong about that

An earlier draft said the job feed was empty in production. A capture of the running APK contradicts it: real postings from CCBRT, CRDB Bank, Ramada and others, each with Lenga CV yangu and Fungua tangazo. What the capture also showed is more useful — not one card carried a match score, because a new account has no career profile, so the unmatched list is the default. See _device/device-07-jobs-live.png. Two further corrections came from the device: the real tab label is CV zangu (not CV) and the real quick action is Ukaguzi wa ATS (not Ukaguzi wa CV). Both are fixed in the mockups.

WHAT I COULD NOT VERIFY

Onboarding animation on the device, and a live chat turn

The onboarding slides and first-run steps could only be re-recorded by clearing app data on the owner’s live phone, which I did not do — so the welcome audit is from source for those two screens and from pixels only for the cold start. I also did not send a message to the assistant, so the chat mockup is a design proposal rather than a live before/after. No claim in this proposal is a device measurement unless it says so.
