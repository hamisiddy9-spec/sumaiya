BEFORE · sumaiya-chat-screen.tsx

The first screen is a decision grid, not an invitation

INITIAL_QUICK_ACTIONS puts four parallel chips in front of a user who has not said anything yet, next to a free-text composer that already affords free typing. Two of the chips duplicate what the composer does; the screen reads as a menu, so a first-time user picks instead of talking.

THE CHANGE · sumaiya-chat-screen.tsx

Cut to the two chips that produce the two artefacts

  • Keep the two that end in a document the user can send: a tailored CV and a cover letter. Both are the app's actual value (job-apply-screen.tsx).
  • Replace the “free chat” chip with nothing — the composer is right there.
  • Replace the “My CV” chip with the in-thread result card below: the artefact shows up where the conversation produced it, instead of in a third place.
ADDED · motion standard §4,”typing stays instant“

The composer must stay still

No animation may wrap the onChangeText path. The chip row scrolls with the platform scroller; it is not a scroll-linked reveal. The result card above enters at 300ms ease.out with a 12px rise — it is the one new arrival on the screen, so it is the one thing allowed to arrive.

HONEST · the mechanism is never the pitch

DeepSeek is named once, in the smallest type on the screen

Product AI is DeepSeek and stays DeepSeek. The note under the composer is a trust line (“usikubali kuweka namba yako ya siri”), not a sales line — the app sells the outcome, and the model name is disclosure rather than feature.
