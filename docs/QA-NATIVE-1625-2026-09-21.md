## 1. COLD START — verdict: **WORKS**

**What I did.** Three runs.
- Attempt 1: `logcat -c`; `am force-stop pro.sumaiya.app`; `am start -W -n pro.sumaiya.app/.MainActivity`;
  capture at +1s/+3s/+8s. The +1s frame came back as the **Android notification shade**
  (`ARTIFACT_shade_at_1s_attempt1.png`) — the shade had been left open before the app launched.
  That frame is a measurement artifact, not an app screen; I discarded it and re-ran.
- Attempt 2 (`coldstart2.sh`): collapsed the shade first (`cmd statusbar collapse`), went home,
  force-stopped, relaunched, captured with millisecond timestamps.
- Attempt 3 (`burst.sh`): launched without `-W` and fired five back-to-back `exec-out screencap`s
  to catch the pre-mount frames.

**Evidence.**

`am start -W` on a genuine cold start, both attempts:
```

TotalTime: 184        (attempt 2; 189 on attempt 1)
```

**Screen text, read off the real screencaps (`vision` on the PNG, not a dump):**

- `burst_0.png` (**815 ms**) — the app's own splash: a soft pink circular badge holding the
  pink tri-lobe mark, centred on a near-white page, with a pale pink radial glow at the top.
  **No text.** This is a branded splash, not an unbranded blank white frame — but note the
  splash background is **light** while the app is **dark**, so a cold start flashes bright before
  settling dark.
- `burst_1.png` (**1441 ms**) — home screen already rendered, mid fade-in (content slightly dimmed).
- `coldstart_1s.png` (**1940 ms**) — home screen fully legible: `Habari za leo asubuhi,` /
  `Backup 1`; card `IMEPENDEKEZWA 1/5` `Anza CV yako ya Huduma kwa mteja kwa muundo wa Apex` /
  `Ujuzi wa kukuza: Huduma kwa wateja · Mawasiliano · Microsoft Excel` / `Ona muundo >`;
  tiles `CV zako 0`, `Jenga CV yako` / `Tumia wasifu kuanza →`, and `Shughuli za haraka`:
  `Sumaiya`, `Tafuta kazi`, `Ukaguzi wa CV`, `Maombi`, `Barua ya maombi`, `TemplateHub`;
  section `CV za hivi karibuni` still showing **grey skeleton placeholders**.
  Bottom nav: `Nyumbani · CV zako · Sumaiya · Pakia · Wasifu`.
- `coldstart_3s.png` (**3707 ms**) — same screen; the CV list is **still skeleton**.
- `coldstart_8s.png` (**8699 ms**) — skeleton resolved to the empty state:

**Logs.** `adb logcat -b crash -d` → **empty output** (no crash records).
`adb logcat -d | grep -i -E 'ReactNativeJS|FATAL|AndroidRuntime'` → exactly one line, no error:
```
09-21 06:34:53.361  8346  8388 I ReactNativeJS: Running "main"
```

**Verdict: WORKS.** I ran a cold start and saw a usable home screen within 1.9 s of launch
(`TotalTime` 184 ms), no crash buffer entry, no `FATAL`, no stuck splash.
**Honest limits:** the earliest frame I could grab was 815 ms (screencap latency ~700 ms), so I
cannot claim what rendered between 0 and 815 ms; I saw the branded splash at 815 ms and the home
screen at 1441 ms. The only defect observed is cosmetic: the CV list shows skeleton rows for
between ~1.9 s and somewhere before 8.7 s — i.e. the home screen is *legible* well before it is
*settled*, and the placeholder look is close enough to a real row that a user could misread it.

**Addendum — a real, non-fatal render warning on cold start.** `logcat -d --pid=8869` (the cold-start
process) contains **211** occurrences of these lines, clustered between 06:35:58.677 and .873
(the splash → home transition):
```
W Reanimated: synchronouslyUpdateUIProps failed for tag 40
W Reanimated: java.lang.reflect.InvocationTargetException
W Reanimated: Caused by: com.facebook.react.bridge.RetryableMountingLayerException:
              Unable to find SurfaceMountingManager for tag: [40]
```
Distinct tags involved: `[28] [30] [40] [62] [64] [74]`. Separately,
`W unknown:ViewManagerPropertyUpdater: Could not find generated setter for class
com.swmansion.reanimated.view.REASharedTransitionBoundaryManager`.
`grep -c 'ReactNativeJS: .*Error'` → **0**. So: the home screen renders and is usable, but the
Reanimated shared-transition layer is updating props against views the Fabric mounting manager
has already torn down, 211 times, on a single cold start. No user-visible symptom was seen at
these three capture points; I am recording it as a signal, not as a confirmed break.

## 2. THE CHAT SURFACE — verdict so far: **PARTIALLY WORKS — the rail works, the assistant does not answer**

### 2.0 Method note — a dump is not the device, demonstrated live
- `adb shell uiautomator dump` on the chat screen returns **`ERROR: could not get idle state.`** —
  the chat never reaches idle. **No dump-based check is possible on this screen at all.**
- Worse, when a dump *did* return while the real screen was the chat, it returned the contents of a
  **stale, hidden WebView** — `'Sign In / Providers | Authentication | Supabase'`, `'Email auth icon'`,
  `'Enable email provider'`, and an `android.widget.Switch` at `[51,556][127,600]`. The screenshot
  taken at that same moment (`exp_B_sumaiya_from_jobs.png`) was the **chat transcript**. The dump and
  the device disagreed completely. Every finding below is taken from a real screencap read with vision.
- The dump was used only as a *locator* for touch coordinates, and every coordinate derived from a dump
  was validated by tapping it and reading the resulting screencap. Two dump-derived coordinates were
  **wrong** and were corrected by measurement off the pixels (see below).

### 2.1 How I got a message in (and two coordinate corrections)
- The previous sweep's exact test message was **already in the transcript**, sent from the owner's
  session: user bubble `Sina CV bado — nisaidie kutengeneza.` + a file chip `Alexandra_Reed_-__CV.docx`,
  then user bubble `Nisaidie kuandika muhtasari mfupi wa CV ya huduma kwa mteja.`
- Nav tab `Sumaiya` bounds per dump were `[541,2783][803,2931]` → I tapped `(672,2857)`. **First tap
  produced nothing; a later identical tap produced the Profile screen.** Taps are not reliably
  delivered to the RN tab bar at this y.
- The composer: the dump-derived y was wrong. I cropped the real pixels
  (`composer_typed_a.png`, region `[0,2550,1344,2850]`) and measured the input field as roughly
  `y 2625–2740`, **centre ≈ (677, 2682)**. Taps at 2750 had been landing in the dead gap between the
  composer and the nav bar — that is why the field never focused. Tapping `(677,2682)` worked:
  `dumpsys input_method` flipped `mInputShown=false` → **`mInputShown=true`**.
- I typed the message with `input text`. First attempt the field read
  `Nisaidie kuandika muhtasari mfupi wa CV pya kuhudumia wateja.` — **my own payload was mistyped**
  (`CV%spya%s` for `CV%sya%s`, and `kuhudumia wateja` for `huduma kwa mteja`). I cleared it
  (`input keyevent 123` then 70× `keyevent 67`) and retyped. Verified off the pixels
  (`composer_typed_clean.png`, region `[80,1795,1310,1935]`) the field then read **exactly**:
  ```
  Nisaidie kuandika muhtasari mfupi wa CV ya huduma kwa mteja.
  ```
- Send: `input tap 1265 1886`. Frames captured continuously into `run/f_NN.png` (30 frames over 35 s,
  each timestamped).

### 2.3 Language and voice
Reply text, read verbatim off `f_29.png` / `receipt_expanded2.png`:
```
Nimekuelewa 😊 Hivi karibuni tumezungumza jambo kama hili — tuchukue hatua inayofuata:
naweza kukagua CV yako, kuilinganisha na kazi unayotaka, au kutengeneza hati mpya. Ni ipi?
```
- **Language: Swahili. WORKS.** Every reply line in the transcript is Swahili. One earlier reply
  contains untranslated terms — `customer service`, and an ATS explanation that is mostly English
  jargon (`"ats" — sijaelewa vizuri. Unamaanisha "ATS", mfumo wa kuchuja CV kwenye kampuni?`).
- **First person: HOLDS.** `Nimekuelewa`, `naweza kukagua CV yako`, `Ninalifikiri ombi lako`,
  `Nimeipokea ombi lako`, `Nitakuandikia muhtasari mfupi…` are all first-person about herself. I did
  **not** find a place where she speaks of herself or of a "service" in the third person.
- **Note:** the possessive flipped from `yangu` (earlier session: `Fungua CV yangu`, `Kagua ATS`) to
  `yako` (this turn: `Fungua CV yako`, `Kagua CV yako`) for the same chips. Two different wordings
  for the same button across sessions is a copy inconsistency, not a voice break.

### 2.4 THE REAL DEFECT — she does not do the thing she is asked to do
The request was *"Nisaidie kuandika muhtasari mfupi wa CV ya huduma kwa mteja."* — write me a short
CV summary for customer service. I ran it and saw **no summary written, twice**:
- The prior session's identical request was answered with a deflection:
  `Umekwisha niambia hivyo, asante! Hebu tuende hatua inayofuata — gusa hapa chini: ukaguzi wa CV, linganisha kazi, au tengeneza hati. Tutafanya nini kwanza?`
- My fresh send was answered with another deflection:
  `Nimekuelewa 😊 Hivi karibuni tumezungumza jambo kama hili — tuchukue hatua inayofuata: naweza kukagua CV yako, kuilinganisha na kazi unayotaka, au kutengeneza hati mpya. Ni ipi?`

Both replies acknowledge the request and then hand a menu of *other* actions back to the user. The
CV summary — the one concrete deliverable asked for — never appears. The rail reports
`✓ Hii hapa jibu lako` ("here is your answer") for a turn whose answer is a question. **This is the
finding that matters most on this surface.**
