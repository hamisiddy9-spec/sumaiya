# Is the CV the MVP? — research ruling, 2026-09-21

**Question:** *"sio bila malipo tu, is CV the MVP?"*
**Ruling: NO.** The CV is one deliverable of the MVP and the one we already have. The MVP is the
**complete application for one advertised job** — and the half we have never produced is the half
Tanzanian employers *mandate*.

---

## The evidence (six independent sources, all checked today)

### 1. The artefact history — the product's entire output, ever

```
cv                  7   (7 distinct owners, newest 2026-09-20)
user_profile       15   more people complete a profile than produce a CV
document_version    1   the entire edit history of the product is ONE row
cover_letter        0
application         0   nobody has ever tracked an application
job_recommendation  0
job              1304   (scraped feed)
```

The CV is the only artefact anyone has produced. It is also **never revised** (one version row),
and **nothing downstream exists**: no letter, no tracked application, no match.

### 2. Our own sales copy contradicts our own funnel

Homepage, three steps: **1. Bandika tangazo la kazi** → 2. Jaza maelezo yako → 3. Pakua PDF yako.
The advert is step one.

The declared activation funnel is `signed_up · onboarded · profile_completed · cv_created ·
cv_exported · application_created · template_selected`. **The advert step does not appear.** We
cannot measure the moment our own copy leads with.

### 3. What Tanzanian employers actually require — primary sources

From official adverts (`ajira.go.tz`, `jsc.go.tz`), an application is a **package**, not a CV:

- **"Barua ya maombi ya kazi iliyosainiwa"** — a *signed* application letter is mandatory
  ("Barua ya Maombi iandikwe kwa Kiswahili au Kiingereza")
- **"Maelezo binafsi yanayojitosheleza (Detailed CV)"** — including **three named referees with
  phone numbers** and a passport photo
- Certified certificate copies, birth certificate or NIDA
- Submitted **only** through `portal.ajira.go.tz` — *"Nakala ngumu hazitapokelewa"* (hard copies
  are not accepted)

**No employer accepts a CV alone.** The letter is not optional and not decorative.

### 4. Market structure — who a CV is even for

```
Formal employment  4.07M (+9.6%/yr) · 450-500k new formal jobs a year
Entering market    800k-1M youth a year  →  a 300-550k job gap
Informal           71.8% of the workforce (25.95M) — mostly never needs a CV
Youth              61% of formal employment; youth unemployment 10% vs 6.2% national
Average wage       TZS 609,354/month
```

A CV is a **formal-sector instrument**. The addressable audience is the ~500k formal openings a
year and the young people chasing them — which also puts the 1,000 TZS single-document price at
**0.16% of a month's average wage**.

### 5. Both direct competitors already ship the letter

- **UndaCV** — CV **+ "Tengenerate Barua"** (cover letter), Swahili form in → quality English CV
  out, PDF + Word, multiple CVs tailored per job, names ATS openly.
- **CV Chap Chap** — browser-only (*"hauhitaji kupakua app"*), and its blog covers *barua ya kuomba
  kazi Tanzania*.

The letter is **table stakes in this market**, and our `cover_letter` table is zero.

### 6. Independent expert panel (OpenRouter `:free`, two roles returned)

> **Product strategist:** *"NO. The MVP is a single-file Application Package Generator … A
> standalone `cv_exported` predicts nothing; 7 CVs exist and 0 applications were created."*
>
> **Tanzanian recruitment professional:** *"NO. The MVP is the signed application letter — the one
> artefact Tanzanian employers cannot reject, that the product currently ships zero of, and that
> competitors already bundle for free … The CV is a distraction: it is the artefact nobody edits,
> nobody completes alongside, and which no Tanzanian employer accepts alone."*

---

## The ruling

**The MVP is: paste or photograph a job advert → get the complete, correctly-addressed application
for that job.** Concretely, for one advert:

1. **The application letter** — addressed to the named officer, naming the position and
   where the advert was seen, Swahili or English. *(Build this first. It is the missing half and
   the market mandates it.)*
2. **The CV**, with the referee block and photo the adverts require.
3. **The checklist** — what THIS advert demands (certificates, NIDA, photo, referees) and where it
   must be submitted. Nobody in the market does this, and it is free to build because we already
   read the advert.

**The event that predicts a returning, recommending user** is not `cv_created`. It is
**`application_package_exported`** — one download that maps 1:1 to what the employer asked for.

## CORRECTION, same day — the letter is built; it is WALLED

The section above says the letter is "the missing half". **That is wrong, and the code says so.**
Checking the flow rather than trusting the DB count:

- The home screen carries a **"Barua ya maombi" tile** that navigates to `JobApply` with
  `{ want: "letter" }` — the advert generator, pre-set to letter. One tap from home.
- The web has dedicated routes: `/editor/cover-letter/[id]`, `/editor/cover-letter/try`.
- The server can write it: `lib/api/routes/cover-letter.ts:62` → `.insert(coverLetter)`.

So the letter exists and is reachable. **The real finding is why nothing was ever written:**

`applications.ts` and `cover-letter.ts` are **entirely** `getUserId → 401` guarded, and the home
screen deliberately hides those tiles from a guest:

> *"Job matches, applications and cover letters are all written on the server against an account,
> so showing them here would be four doors into an invitation."*

```js
guestQuickActions = quickActions.filter(a => ["sumaiya","shield-check","layout-grid"].includes(a.icon))
```

**Which explains the whole data set at once:**

| | reachable by a guest? | rows |
|---|---|---|
| CV editor | **yes** — on-device | **7** |
| advert → letter / application | **no** — account + server | **0** / **0** |

**The differentiated flow is behind a wall; the commodity is open.** And it cuts against the
standing rule that *a guest must finish and download before being asked for an account*: a guest can
finish a **CV**, but cannot finish an **application**, because the letter needs the server.

### What that changes about the fix

Not "build the letter" — **un-wall it.** The cheapest honest route is a **no-AI letter generated
on the device**: the advert already states the position, the addressee and the reference, which is
everything a structurally correct letter needs. That is offline, costs nothing per use, satisfies
what the adverts mandate, and leaves AI polish as the account/premium upsell rather than as the toll
gate on the MVP.


## What this says to stop building

- **More CV templates.** 47 curated already; nobody revises their CV (1 version row).
- **The scraped job feed as a headline feature.** 1,304 jobs, 0 applications. The panel's word for
  it is "decoration". Keep it as context for the advert flow, not as a destination.
- **The profile wizard as a gate.** 15 profiles produced 7 CVs; it is a toll booth on the way to
  the thing that matters.
- **Challenged by name:** the strategist says cut the *invite-friends-for-premium* loop too —
  *"viral loops fail when the core artefact doesn't win interviews; users don't share broken
  tools."* That is a direct challenge to the premium model chosen this week, and it deserves an
  answer: it is not an argument against sharing, it is an argument for **shipping the letter first**,
  because a sharing loop amplifies whatever it carries.

## The cheapest test that would settle it (~$50, one week)

**Concierge, over WhatsApp.** Recruit 20 real job seekers (university and TVET WhatsApp groups).
They forward a job advert; you hand back the complete package by hand, within 15 minutes. Measure
how many **actually submit** and how many get a **callback**.

That measures the only thing that matters — whether the artefact wins interviews — before a line
of the letter feature is written.

Second, cheaper still: take 20 users who signed up and never returned, put a real `ajira.go.tz`
advert in front of them, and ask them to prepare a full application **on paper**. Watch which step
they stall at. That is where the product is broken.

## What this research cannot tell us

- **Whether we have a letter problem or a demand problem.** `cover_letter = 0` could mean nobody
  wanted one, or that the flow is unreachable or broken. Nothing in the data distinguishes those.
  The paper test above distinguishes them in an afternoon.
- **Whether Swahili-in / English-out is required or merely nice.** Both competitors offer it; the
  adverts accept either language. Unmeasured.
- **Whether anyone will pay anything.** All prices in this document are proposals, not observations.

## Correction to the panel, for the record

The strategist read the profile figure as *"profile wizard (15 rows, 0 CVs)"*. That is wrong — 15
profiles sit alongside **7** CVs, not zero. The conclusion it drew (the profile is not the product)
still holds, but the number it cited does not, and it should not be repeated.
