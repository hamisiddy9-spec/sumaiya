# Sumaiya — Ad Script v3 (bilingual, voice + Veo)

9:16 vertical · ~30–37s · Swahili VO with the English terms people already use
(*stationery, AI, PDF, download, apply*). This version matches the product's real
flow: AI writes the CV **and cover letter**, tailors to the pasted job advert,
exports a PDF, the user sends it to the employer; start free, 1,000 TSh/day.

| # | Line | English meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Bado unalipa elfu mbili stationery… kuandika CV tu?** | Still paying 2,000 at a stationery shop just to *type* a CV? |
| 2 | **Sumaiya inakuandikia CV yako kwa AI — na barua ya kazi pia.** | Sumaiya writes your CV with AI — and the cover letter too. |
| 3 | **Inaandika kila kitu kuendana na kazi unayoomba.** | It writes everything to match the job you're applying for. |
| 4 | **Weka tangazo la kazi, app itengeneze document, download PDF.** | Paste the job advert, the app builds the document, download the PDF. |
| 5 | **Kisha umtumie mwajiri — unaomba haraka zaidi.** | Then send it to the employer — you apply faster. |
| 6 | **Kuanza ni bure. Kwa siku, buku moja tu.** | Starting is free. Per day, just one "buku" (1,000). |
| 7 | **Pakua Sumaiya. Sumaiya dot app slash pakua.** | Download Sumaiya. sumaiya.app/pakua |

Pronounce English terms naturally; say **ATS** as a normal acronym; say the URL
"sumaiya dot app slash pakua".
