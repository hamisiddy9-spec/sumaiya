## Recording tips
- Quiet room, phone Voice Memo / recorder, phone held about a hand-span from your mouth.
- Ad energy: upbeat, confident, slightly up-tempo. Smile on line 4 (the payoff).
- **Best:** 7 separate short clips named `1`…`7` (or `0`…`6`). Easiest to sync perfectly.
- **Also fine:** one take, all 7 lines, ~1s pause between each.
- Send the audio file(s) in chat (`.m4a` / `.mp3` / `.wav`). We clean + level + drop it
  into the ad, replacing the placeholder text-to-speech voice.
