**2. Swahili: correctness, warmth, ambiguity**

- `"Miundo 7 bora — sidebars za rangi, pete za picha, mistari ya muda"`  
  **Reads wrong:** "pete za picha" = "picture rings" (jewellery). You mean *picha zinazozunguka* or *viringo vya picha*. "Mistari ya muda" = "lines of time" — a literal calque of "timelines". Say *mstari wa historia* or *ratiba ya kazi*.

- `"Inapatikana kwa kualika marafiki — siku 7 kila mmoja akijenga CV"`  
  **Ambiguous:** *kualika* is "to invite/call", but a second-language reader hears *kuuliza* (to ask). Use *kuwasilisha* (refer) or *kuwapeleka*. Also *kila mmoja akijenga* — "each one builds" — missing *yake* (their own). Write: *"Inapatikana kwa kuwasilisha marafiki — siku 7 kila mmoja anapojenga CV yake ya kwanza."*

- `"Kozi za kina: umahiri wa usaili, majadiliano ya mshahara"`  
  **Cold.** *Umahiri* is academic "proficiency"; *ujumbe* or *ustadi* feels human. *Majadiliano* = "discussions", not "negotiation". Use *uchambuzi wa mshahara* or *jinsi ya kupata mshahara bora*.

- `"Uamuzi ulioandikwa kuhusu CV yako, kwa lugha rahisi"`  
  **Stiff.** *Uamuzi* = verdict/judgment (court tone). Say *Maoni ya kina* or *Uchambuzi wa moja kwa moja*. *Lugha rahisi* = "simple language" — better: *Kiswahili rahisi* or *maneno ya kawaida*.

- `"Alama ya CV yako ya uhakika"`  
  **Nonsense.** "Alama ya uhakika" = "mark of certainty". You mean *alama ya ATS* or *alama ya ufanisi*. Drop *ya uhakika*.

- `"Tutakujulisha malipo yanapofunguliwa"`  
  **Presumes opening.** If payments never open, you never notify. Say *"Tutakutumia arifa ikiwa malipo yataanza"*.
