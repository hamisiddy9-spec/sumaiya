You are the HEAD OF GROWTH at a competitor. Pick the competitor you would actually
fear if you were this product: an incumbent African job board with real listings and
real traffic (for example Zoom Tanzania / BrighterMonday East Africa / Jobberman /
Fuzu), or a global AI CV builder with a paid acquisition budget. Say which one you
are, and why that is the threatening one.

Read `docs/research/rebrand-2026-09-20/CONTEXT-BRIEF.md` in this repo so you know
what the product actually is (including the honest weaknesses: the jobs feed measured
empty, payments off, one-person team, Swahili-first).

Then answer as the competitor:

1. What are you AFRAID of about this product, if anything? Be specific and measured —
   if you would dismiss it, say that and why.
2. If you wanted to kill it, what would you do? Name the cheapest two or three moves.
   This is the most useful part of your answer for the other side, so be concrete.
3. What is the ONE thing this product has that you cannot copy quickly, and what is
   the ONE thing it lacks that you will exploit?
4. Its public claim is currently `CV inayokufikisha kwa mwajiri` — "the CV that reaches
   the employer". If it moved to a much wider claim (a "career intelligence platform"
   or "job-search operating system" spanning CV, jobs, tracking, interview prep and
   learning), what would you do differently — and would that wider claim make it
   easier or harder for you to beat? Answer the counter-intuitive part honestly: does
   a wider positioning actually HELP an incumbent?
5. What should it lead with, from the standpoint of someone who would rather it did
   not? (Sometimes the honest answer is "the narrow thing nobody else does well.")

Where you are guessing, label it JUDGEMENT. No invented statistics or fake traffic
numbers. Cite a real source URL if you use a real fact about a real competitor.

Write your answer as markdown to `docs/research/rebrand-2026-09-20/pass-4-competitor-growth.md`.
