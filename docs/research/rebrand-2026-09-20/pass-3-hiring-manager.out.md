Key take: a mention of the app is a mild negative on its own (signals formatting help, not substance) unless the CV itself reads as specific; "reaches the employer" is not honest since nothing in the product touches the employer side — it's a readiness claim wearing a delivery claim; and at scale the tool's biggest risk is making candidates interchangeable, which rewards weak candidates and erases signal for strong ones.
