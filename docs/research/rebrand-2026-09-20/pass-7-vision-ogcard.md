## 1. Exact transcription

- **Sumaiya**
- **CV inayokupa ajira — kwa AI**

## 2. Who is this card for?

**JUDGEMENT:** This card is for **Swahili-speaking job seekers** who already understand “CV” and are open to using AI to improve their chances of employment.

**JUDGEMENT:** It is clearly not for:

- English-only readers.
- People unfamiliar with the term “CV.”
- People who need proof, screenshots, testimonials, or a concrete example before trusting a product.
- People who do not already understand what Sumaiya does.

## 3. What breaks for an English reader?

**JUDGEMENT:** Almost everything breaks.

The English reader can see “Sumaiya,” “CV,” and “AI,” but the actual promise — **“inayokupa ajira”** — is unreadable. They do not know whether this is:

- A CV builder.
- A job board.
- An AI writing tool.
- A recruitment service.
- A paid or free product.
- Tanzania-specific or global.

The card gives them no reason, context, or action.

## 4. Call to action judgement

**JUDGEMENT:** There is no real CTA.

The line **“CV inayokupa ajira — kwa AI”** is a value claim, not an instruction. It may create mild interest for a Swahili-speaking job seeker, but it does not tell them what to do next.

Missing specifically:

- **“Bure” / “Free.”**
- **“Anza” / “Start.”**
- **“Tengeneza CV” / “Create your CV.”**
- A WhatsApp or phone-based action.
- A clear reason to tap now.
- Any indication of speed, ease, or outcome.

A stranger may wonder, “What happens if I tap?”

## 5. Restraint or wasted impression?

**JUDGEMENT:** Mostly wasted impression.

Minimalism can work for a known brand, but this is a **first-touch WhatsApp share card**. On this surface, the product has seconds to explain itself. The current card spends the impression on mood and brand presence, but not on conversion.

It does not answer the urgent questions:

- What is it?
- Is it free?
- Is it for me?
- Is it in Tanzania?
- Can I use it without paying online?
- Will it help me get work?
- What do I do next?

A device mockup, example CV, “Bure,” WhatsApp cue, or job-seeker proof would likely convert better than extra empty space.

## 6. Better card copy

**Swahili first:**

**Tengeneza CV yako ya ajira kwa dakika 5 — Bure**  
**Anza kwenye WhatsApp**

**English:**

**Build your job-ready CV in 5 minutes — Free**  
**Start on WhatsApp**

Alternative if keeping the AI mechanism:

**Tengeneza CV yako ya ajira kwa AI — Bure**  
**Anza sasa kwenye WhatsApp**

## 7. Does “kwa AI” earn its place?

**JUDGEMENT:** Not on this surface.

“Kwa AI” is a mechanism, not the user’s main desire. A 24-year-old job seeker likely cares more about:

- Getting a CV.
- Getting interviews.
- Doing it free.
- Doing it quickly.
- Using WhatsApp or a phone.
- Avoiding payment friction.

AI may help credibility with some users, but here it weakens clarity. The card should lead with **job outcome + free + easy action**, then mention AI only if space remains.
