# YOUR JOB

Answer, in this order, quoting the exact string you are criticising each time:

1. **In the first 5 seconds, what do you think this product is?** Answer as the Dar es Salaam visitor, in
   Swahili, in one sentence. Then say which line on the page made you think it, and which line contradicted it.

2. **What is confusing?** Every string where a person would have to re-read it, or would not know what happens
   after they tap. Quote it and say what the confusion is in plain terms.

3. **What is generic?** Every string that could belong to any CV product in any country. Quote it. Say what
   specific fact was replaced by a general claim.

4. **Which claims are FEATURES masquerading as BENEFITS?** For each: quote it, name the mechanism it is really
   selling, and rewrite it as the outcome the person actually gets. Be strict — "47+ templates" is a count,
   not a benefit.

5. **What would a first-time Tanzanian visitor NOT understand?** Words, concepts, English loanwords, and
   anything that assumes knowledge they do not have. Include anything that reads as Kenyan, Western, or
   foreign. Quote it and say what they would think instead.

6. **What does the page FAIL TO SAY at all?** The absences. Specifically consider: whether it works without
   internet, what happens on a slow connection, whether it works on a basic Android phone, where the jobs come
   from, who the employers are, what happens after you download the CV, how they make money, whether Swahili is
   real Swahili or translated English, and whether the product is actually available in their country.

7. **Trust problems.** Anything that would make you doubt it. Include the sample CV's identity, the
   testimonials, the money-back guarantee, the guarantee that CVs "pass" employer screening, and anything on
   the page that cannot be verified by the reader.

8. **If you could keep only ONE line from this page and had to delete the rest, which one and why?** Then:
   **write what the hero of this page SHOULD say**, in Swahili first, for this specific reader. Give three
   competing options and say which you would ship and why. Keep every one of them under 12 words. No
   exclamation marks. No hype. No "AI".

9. **Rank the page's sections** by how much they help a stranger decide to trust it, worst first. Say which
   sections should be deleted entirely and why.

Be blunt. Do not be polite about weaknesses, and do not soften a criticism into a suggestion. Where you are
guessing, say "this is my guess".
