## 1. The market reality (East Africa)

| Fact | Source | So what for Sumaiya |
|---|---|---|
| Digital payments in Kenya = **M-Pesa**, not cards. Everyday spend runs through mobile money. | World Bank, CGAP | Play Store billing (card-first) will convert badly. Plan for **M-Pesa / mobile-money** checkout (Pesapal, Flutterwave, Paystack) even if v1 uses Play billing. |
| **Willingness to pay for content/subscriptions is low**; Western subscription models "a difficult import". | Reuters Institute, Nieman Lab | A $10–20/mo sub (Enhancv, Novoresume, Zety pricing) is a non-starter here. |
| Publishers that made paid work used **freemium + micropayments as a gateway** — a low-commitment first payment to build the *habit* of paying, then convert to a light sub. | Nieman Lab (Kenya case) | Sumaiya's premium should have a **very cheap one-off unlock** as the front door, not only a subscription. |
| Mobile data is **expensive relative to income**; users prefer things that load fast / work intermittently. | Reuters Institute | Sumaiya is already offline-first — lean into it as a *selling point*, not just an implementation detail. |

### Competitors seen tonight

- **Fuzu** (Kenya) — job portal + AI matching + AI resume builder. Big job DB. Freemium; "Fuzu Instant" paid tiers historically ~KES 200–500/mo range for premium coaching/visibility.
- **BrighterMonday** — job board + a **human** CV-review/writing service (free basic review, paid full rewrite — priced as a service, not a product).
- **SwiftKazi**, **Careergo**, **Kompeaa** — newer "AI CV / AI job application for East Africa" apps. Careergo's own description is almost word-for-word Sumaiya's. This space is **filling up fast** — the window to own "the Sumaiya persona" is now.
- Global builders (Enhancv $16.50+, Novoresume $21.99/mo, Zety $25.95/mo, VisualCV $16/mo) — irrelevant on price here, but they set the **feature bar**: templates, ATS score, tailoring, cover letters. Sumaiya already matches or beats this.

**Where Sumaiya is differentiated:** bilingual SW/EN done properly, offline-first, one warm persona instead of a dashboard, built for low-literacy phones, guides written for *this* market. No competitor combines all of these.

## 2. Recommended pricing model

The whole essential product stays **free forever** (CV builder, 4 templates, ATS check, tailoring, cover letters, the short guides, chat). That's the moat — a free tool people tell their friends about.

**Sumaiya Premium** — two ways in, pick whichever the user is comfortable with:

| | Price (indicative) | What it unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| **One-off unlock** | **~KES 150 / ~NGN 1,500 / ~$1** | Executive + Compact templates, permanently. The "gateway" payment. |
| **Premium pass** | **~KES 300–500 / mo** or **~KES 2,000 / yr** | All advanced templates + new ones monthly, the deep courses (salary negotiation, career change, LinkedIn), priority AI (skip any future rate limits), "Sumaiya reviewed your CV" deep audit. |

Rationale: the one-off is the habit-builder Nieman's Kenya case says you need. The annual is where the margin is (data cost per user is tiny; the AI calls are the real COGS — a yearly pass at KES 2,000 covers a lot of DeepSeek tokens).

**Do not gate:** anything that blocks someone from *getting a job*. Gating the ATS check or basic tailoring would be the wrong kind of greedy and would kill word-of-mouth.

**Payment rails, in order:** Play billing first (fastest to ship, no PCI), then add **Pesapal or Flutterwave** M-Pesa/mobile-money for the ~70% who'll bounce off card entry. The `useEntitlements()` hook built tonight is the single seam — swap the local flag for a server check + receipt validation.

## 3. Courses / guides as a product line

Built tonight: 4 free guides + 3 premium stubs, in-app, offline, bilingual.

**If you want this as a real revenue line:**
- The free guides are lead-gen and retention (the home "tip of the day" already surfaces them).
- Premium courses want **more depth**: a multi-step course with a checklist the user works through, maybe a "practice with Sumaiya" turn at the end of each (reuse the interview-coach endpoint). Salary negotiation and interview mastery are the two people will actually pay for.
- **Content cost is the constraint** — these need a real writer (or a careful Sumaiya-assisted draft + human edit). Budget ~1 day per premium course.
- Later: sell individual courses à la carte (KES 100 each) as well as bundled in Premium. Same one-off-vs-pass logic as templates.

## 4. What I'd build next (ranked)

1. **Deploy the assistant prompt fixes + `/api/mobile/build-cv`.** The chat→CV feature is live in code but dead until the web deploys. This is the single biggest UX unlock.
2. **M-Pesa / mobile-money checkout.** Nothing premium earns a cent without it. Pesapal has the simplest KE integration.
3. **Push notifications that matter** — "your CV passed the ATS check for [role]", "3 jobs match your new CV", a weekly guide. The `device-token` endpoint exists; the sending side needs a cron + templates.
4. **Real ATS keyword extraction on-device** was flagged in the audit — the native `matchJd` is a simple bag-of-words. Fine for now; a small upgrade (n-grams, synonyms from the JD) would make the match score trustworthy.
5. **Template thumbnails.** The picker shows text pills; a tiny rendered preview per template (WebView at 60px, or pre-rendered PNGs) makes choosing tactile. Medium effort.
6. **"Sumaiya reviewed your CV" deep audit** — a premium one-tap that runs the full ATS + line findings + a written summary, formatted like a report. High perceived value, reuses code that exists.
7. **Referral loop on native.** The web has `referral.ts`. Native has nothing. "Invite a friend, you both get a premium template" — cheap growth.
8. **WhatsApp entry point.** The `apps/whatsapp` service exists. A "start on WhatsApp, finish in the app" flow meets people where they already are.

## 5. Risks / open questions for the morning

- **Careergo / SwiftKazi** — are these ours, partners, or competitors? Careergo's copy is suspiciously close. Worth 10 minutes to check.
- **AI cost per user** at scale — need a real number. DeepSeek is cheap but `build-cv` + `tailor` + chat per active user adds up. The `AI_USER_MONTHLY_USD` cap exists; pick a value.
- **Content ownership** for premium courses — who writes them, on what cadence.
- **Play Store review** — an app that mentions payment outside Play billing can get rejected. M-Pesa checkout for *digital goods* needs care (physical-goods exemption doesn't apply). May need Play billing as the only in-app option and M-Pesa only via the website.
- The premium tier is **structurally built but sells nothing** — `useEntitlements` returns false always. That's deliberate (no billing) but means the PremiumSheet "notify me" list is the only thing collecting intent right now.
